BANKING · FINTECH
10
engineers led through a legacy-to-microservices migration
With business, product and QARoyal Bank of Canada (via TCS)2022 – 2024

Moving a retail bank's legacy services to secure microservices

Legacy services were hard to scale and change, yet had to carry transfers, statements, alerts and mobile banking under strict security rules.

Decision

  • Spring Boot REST services on AWS with PostgreSQL and Redis
  • OAuth 2.0 and 2FA through RBC's OAuth Broker
  • Message-broker async processing for alerts

Trade-offs

  • Eventual consistency for notifications so slow services never block customers
  • Circuit breakers and retry with backoff to stop cascading failures

Result

  • ACH, wire and C2C transfers on a modern React platform
  • Releases gated by load, stress and API tests

MARKETING SAAS
30%
faster load times, with 15% higher client engagement
With product, design and backendPica9 · Seattle2024 – 2025

A digital asset management module on a modernized front end

Customers managed large asset libraries on an ageing front end, and every product carried its own copy of shared components.

Decision

  • Upgrade to React 19 with reusable components across products
  • DAM with role-based access, search, infinite scroll, bulk actions
  • Docker for every service, images in ECR, assets in S3

Trade-offs

  • Server pagination instead of loading whole libraries
  • Up-front cost of a shared library, repaid in every product

Result

  • 25% fewer production defects
  • Mentored 5–6 engineers on front-end standards

HEALTHCARE
2,000+
healthcare facilities hiring on one platform
With clients and third-party vendorsBacancy Technology2021 – 2022

A high-availability hiring platform for healthcare

Facilities needed to post jobs, screen candidates and sign contracts in one place, with tight control over sensitive data.

Decision

  • React and Spring Boot on MySQL, built for high availability
  • Role-based access for facilities, recruiters and admins

Trade-offs

  • Bought e-signature (Right Signature) rather than build: faster and already compliant
  • Open GSA integration for U.S. government data

Result

  • 20% shorter time to hire
  • One workflow from job post to signed offer

EDUCATION · ERP
22%
faster data retrieval with Elasticsearch
Led cross-functional teamsBrainvire Infotech2018 – 2021

Multi-tenant ERP and university management SaaS

Universities ran grants, IRB reviews and sponsored projects by hand, and many institutions had to share one platform safely.

Decision

  • Multi-tenant microservices on MongoDB and PostgreSQL
  • Elasticsearch search and D3.js dashboards
  • LDAP sign-in for each university's directory

Trade-offs

  • Polyglot storage kept in sync, each store used for what it does best
  • Tenant isolation designed in from day one

Result

  • Automated Grants, IRB and Sponsored Projects workflows
  • AWS infrastructure tuned for availability
